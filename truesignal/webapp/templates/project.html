Workflow

  1. Review the findings grid and mark each one Not Exploitable, Confirmed, or leave it To Verify — like a normal Checkmarx audit pass. Select several at once with the bulk-audit bar.
  2. Once a handful of findings that share a passthrough function are dismissed, run the analysis. TrueSignal proposes sanitizer/source/sink learnings backed by your audits and the code itself.
  3. Approve what you agree with. TrueSignal applies the override and re-scans — you'll see exactly which findings got downgraded (false positives resolved) and whether anything new surfaced (a false negative made visible).
  4. Everything applied is recorded in the ledger for audit and rollback — a wrong learning is never permanent.
